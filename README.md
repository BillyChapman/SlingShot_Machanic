**SlingShot Physics Gym**

Billy Chapman

**Course:** Physics Gameplay Gym

**Engine:** Unreal Engine

A micro-gameplay physics experiment focused on charging mechanics, trajectory prediction and environment interaction. Players charge and launch rocks from a slingshot to knock bottles over. 

**Controls:**

          - E to Pick up SlingShot
          - Left Mouse Button to fire rock, holding will increase distance
          - W,A,S,D to traverse the environment

**Key Gameplay Mechanics: **

          - Power charge system: Measures real-time input duration to impact launch
          - Trajectory Prediction: An accurate feedback line allows players to predict their launch
          - Physics Impact & Collisions: Projectiles use power and collision to interact with objects

**Aim:** Using the Slingshot provided, knock down all bottles located throughout the map
